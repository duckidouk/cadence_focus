## Current assets and liabilities

### Plain-English meaning

Current assets are assets expected to become cash within the normal operating cycle.

Current liabilities are amounts due within one year.

### Important formula

Current liabilities = Current assets - Net current assets

### SCCI-GROUP example

2025 current assets: £14,205,292  
2025 net current position: £(3,707,458)  
Derived current liabilities: £17,912,750

### Notes to self ###

- Current creditors equal current liabilities in this SCCI filing.
- Not every liability represents financial leverage.
- The same XBRL value can appear under multiple contexts.
- Duplicate contexts must not be added together.
