Cadence Focus KPI Analysis Process

1. Define the Business Question
Decide what decision the analysis should support and what Pieter needs to understand.

2. Create the KPI Dictionary
Define each KPI, its formula, comparison period, source, and display rule before coding.

3. Build the SQL Reporting Dataset
Validate and transform raw data into one controlled row per company and reporting period.

4. Calculate and Validate in Python
Calculate changes and KPIs in Pandas, then test for missing values, duplicates, and incorrect entities.

5. Create the Plotly Charts
Turn the validated results into clear, decision-focused charts without exaggerating the evidence.

6. Assemble the Streamlit Dashboard
Combine KPI cards, charts, controls, sources, and data-quality warnings into one usable page.

7. Write the Analyst Conclusions
Explain the important movements, limitations, and management questions without making unsupported claims.
